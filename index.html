How old and how far is NGC 1245?

NASA SEES · UT Austin Center for Space Research · Jun – Sep 2023
Python, NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, AstroImageJ, Stellarium

Our team measured the age and distance of an open star cluster in Perseus using telescope images from the SARA observatory in the Canary Islands. I wrote Python scripts to clean and process raw data from 10 universities, 3 telescopes and 500 stars, then used photometry and a color-magnitude diagram to date the cluster.
